MFDCO v0.28 references and scope

Historical district names:
Berlin (September 1, 1945), German History in Documents and Images.
Original cartography: IEG-MAPS, A. Kunz (2004); revision Gabriel Moss (2021).
https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/occupation-and-the-emergence-of-two-states-1945-1961/berlin-september-1-1945
Only 20 district names and approximate center placements are included as editable labels.
No source map image is redistributed. Coordinates are approximate modern anchors,
not surveyed historical boundaries, streets, buildings, or urban extents.

Weapon glyph reference context:
NHHC NH 123897: streamlined naval gun mount.
https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/nhhc/our-collections/photography/numerical-list-of-images/nhhc-series/nh-series/NH-123000/NH-123897.html
Australian War Memorial 302662: external gun mounts and torpedo tubes, USS Mahan.
https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/302662
All UI glyphs are independently drawn generic SVG forms. No source photos are bundled.
Modern VLS, CIWS, missile, and laser graphics are schematic, not model-accurate replicas.
All simulation values are fictional; no real weapon performance data is provided.
