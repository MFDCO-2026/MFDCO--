No reference images are redistributed. Labels and facility records are original structured data.
Geographic anchors, road alignments, rectangular footprints and urban pattern extents are
manual approximations for editing, NOT surveyed historic geometry or individual house layouts.
Historic existence and geographical precision are separate: every record says approximate.
The underlying modern DEM/coastline is unchanged. Terrain is not affected by visual waterways.
No Ford Island modern bridge/memorial or post-battle Sand Island runway is added.
All facilities have neutral teams and zero added defense/damage/support effects by default.
Each pack has stable IDs, a dated source list, and version 1.0.0 for later corrections.
Movement timelines are a future stage; no historical force movement is claimed here.
