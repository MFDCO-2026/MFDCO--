MFDCO Tools v3.7 Architecture
================================

1. Symbolic / algebra module
----------------------------
New:
mfdco-symbolic.js

Integer operations:
- primeFactors
- factorInteger
- simplifyFraction

One-variable polynomial CAS:
- expandPoly
- collectPoly
- simplifyPoly
- factorPoly
- polyDegree
- polyCoefficients
- polyDerivative
- polyIntegral

Series:
- taylorCoefficients
- taylor
- maclaurin

Taylor/Maclaurin uses truncated power-series arithmetic rather than repeated
finite-difference derivatives. Supported analytic functions include:
sin, cos, tan, sinh, cosh, tanh, exp, ln/log, sqrt, pow.

Trigonometric series use radians, matching standard mathematical convention.

2. Quantity-aware engineering functions
----------------------------------------
When engineering functions receive quantity values, their outputs preserve
and combine dimensions.

Examples:
force(2 kg, 3 m/s^2)       -> 6 N
kineticEnergy(2 kg, 3 m/s) -> 9 J
power(100 J, 5 s)          -> 20 W
ohmV(2 A, 5 ohm)           -> 10 V
stress(1000 N, 0.01 m^2)   -> 100000 Pa

Plain numeric calls remain backward-compatible.

3. String-valued calculations
-----------------------------
The expression engine now permits registered symbolic functions to return
strings, making this valid:

expanded = expandPoly("(x+1)^3", "x")
print(expanded)

Limitations
-----------
- Symbolic polynomial algebra formally supports one variable at a time.
- factorPoly primarily uses rational-root factorization and leaves an
  irreducible remainder in polynomial form.
- Taylor series are truncated to order 24 for safety.

Recommended v3.8

- complex-number support
