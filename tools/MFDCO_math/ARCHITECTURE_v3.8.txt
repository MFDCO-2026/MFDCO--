MFDCO Tools v3.8
================
Adds mfdco-advanced.js: complex numbers, polynomial roots, exact equation helpers,
linear systems, symbolic substitution, general derivative/partial, limits,
statistics/regression, probability distributions, mechanics/orbital/electrical functions.

Templates are expanded to 33 total. New large templates combine for/while,
if/elif, def/return, break/continue, print and plot. They use the existing
one-click template insertion UI.
