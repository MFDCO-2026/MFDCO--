MFDCO Tools v3.6 Architecture
================================

New module: mfdco-quantities.js

Supported:
- 1 km + 500 m
- 100 m / 5 s
- 2 N * 3 m
- 1 kg * 9.80665 m/s^2
- 100 km/h -> m/s

Dimensional analysis:
- addition/subtraction require equal dimensions
- multiplication/division combine dimensions
- quantity comparisons require equal dimensions

Calculator/Code variables:
Variable-row units are now passed into the evaluator as quantities.

Example:
d = 100 m
t = 5 s
d/t => 20 m/s

Absolute temperatures:
degC/degF/degR can be converted, but ambiguous arithmetic is restricted.

Recommended v3.7:
- quantity-aware engineering function outputs
- temperature-difference arithmetic
- unit-aware vectors/matrices
- unit autocomplete/highlighting
